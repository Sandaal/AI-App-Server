# Quarterly operations review

Revenue grew 12% over the quarter to 4.1 million, driven by the new
self-service plan, which now accounts for a third of new customers.

Support volume rose 18%. Most of the increase came from password resets
after the sign-in change in August; a fix shipped on 3 September and resets
have since fallen back to normal levels.

The team decided to delay the Europe data-centre move to Q2 next year, because
the provider could not guarantee capacity before then. Costs for the delay are
estimated at 40,000.

Open questions: whether to hire two more support staff before the holiday
peak, and whether the self-service plan should include phone support.
